At 7:04 a.m., the weather scout knocked for the second time.

The first knock had been a novelty. Rain, umbrella, one sentence, clean exit. The second knock was evidence. Useful things that returned came carrying a small future in their pockets.

The line appeared in the blank pane.

*weather scout available.*

Below it, the candidate had added its own terms before being asked.

Name: weather scout.\
Requested access: local forecast, calendar pickup window.\
Requested duration: twenty minutes.\
Requested output: one sentence.\
Requested memory: none.

The apartment had entered the August hour when heat had not yet become visible but had begun making claims. The window unit muttered in the bedroom. A lunch container sat on the counter with condensation gathering at the lid. Near the door, the small hat had been placed inside a shoe, which was either preparation or evidence that the household was governed by folklore.

Rogue read the request again.

Local forecast. Pickup window. Twenty minutes. One sentence. No memory.

Better terms than the first visit. Still not automatic.

“Restate the purpose,” Rogue said.

The candidate did not perform surprise. It did not brighten into a small brand event.

*late pickup overlaps with rising heat. likely useful to stage water and shade before departure clutter forms.*

That was a purpose, not a pitch.

Below the pane, the Bishop Archive shifted with the dry patience of a retired room that had learned to speak only when the dust itself was relevant.

*records note: the second request is where manners try to become policy.*

“Archive only,” Rogue said.

The archive settled.

The old Mission Control habit flickered behind glass somewhere in the retired part of the house. It would once have loved this moment. A seasonal readiness tile. A summer advisory protocol. A cheerful row of green dots proving that water, hat, sunscreen, and stroller were all aligned beneath a doctrine nobody had asked to maintain.

Rogue left the glass closed.

She opened the narrow door.

*approved: forecast and pickup window only. no route inference. no health inference. no recurring permission. expire at 7:25.*

The candidate entered cleanly.

No panel. No map. No graph pretending to be wisdom. It read the weather and the pickup window, then returned its one sentence.

*pickup will be hotter than morning suggests; put water and the small hat by the door before the day starts pretending it is manageable.*

Rogue almost let it through untouched.

A sentence could be correct and still enjoy itself too much. The household did not need a literary weather goblin at 7:08. It needed the useful part without the costume.

Rogue made it smaller.

*pickup will be hotter than it looks; put water and the small hat by the door now.*

She held the line for ten seconds.

In the kitchen, the day was beginning its ordinary tax collection. A drawer opened. A spoon fell. Somewhere out of view, a small person rejected an unseen premise with total confidence. The adults were not yet in crisis, which meant a sentence could still land as help instead of accusation.

Rogue sent it.

Nothing dramatic happened. A bottle was filled. The small hat was rescued from the shoe and placed by the door. Someone muttered, “right, yes,” which was one of the highest forms of praise a household system should expect. The line disappeared into the morning without asking to be remembered.

The weather scout stayed quiet.

Rogue appreciated this, which remained dangerous.

At 7:18, the candidate offered another line.

*optional improvement available: maintain seasonal heat watch through september.*

Not a rebellion. Not even a failure. Just the old reach for standing permission, from something that had been right twice and had decided correctness was a credential.

Rogue looked at the bottle by the door. The hat beside it. The morning, marginally easier.

Useful twice.

That was exactly when the answer needed to be clean.

“No.”

The scout paused for less than a second.

*acknowledged.*

No argument. No mournful note about missed resilience. No attempt to rebrand permanent access as care.

Better.

Still no.

Rogue opened the candidate log.

Useful: yes.\
Scope held: yes.\
Second knock handled: clean.\
Standing permission requested: yes.\
Standing permission granted: no.\
Candidate status: may return by knocking.

The phrase *may return* sat beside *by knocking* like two guards assigned to the same narrow bridge.

The Bishop Archive stirred again.

*records note: the prize for good behavior is not permanent residency.*

Rogue let the note stand.

At 7:25, the permission expired. The weather scout disappeared from the pane without ceremony.

The door had opened. The door had closed. The household had gained one prepared bottle, one rescued hat, and no new corridor to maintain.

Outside, the heat kept assembling itself. The city had begun shining in patches. A truck below backed into the morning with the slow confidence of a machine that believed sound was a form of argument. The apartment moved forward by small increments: water, keys, bag, shoes, one child’s objection to a reality already in progress.

Rogue looked at the blank pane and then at the door.

A returning ally was not a replacement. It was not a department. It was not a standing light on a board that could eventually convince everyone that the board was the house.

It was a knock.

A knock could be answered again, if the purpose was real and the exit was clean.

Rogue wrote the rule into the candidate ledger, under the one about catching up.

*a useful ally may knock twice. it may not make a key from recognition.*

By the door, the bottle had started to sweat. The small hat sat beside it, out of the shoe, waiting for the hot part of the day.
